Word from
the architect

Ivan Grekov. Chief architect of KAMEN ARCHITECTS

Likova business center is an exceptional architectural project that complements the dynamic transportation artery of the Borovskii highway intersection with its flowy forms.

This single-building structure with variable height, accentuated shapes, and height transitions simultaneously emblematizes stability and movement. Its central volumes are lifted above the ground and formed into portals that connect exterior and interior spaces. The open-air inner courtyard with an observation terrace will be your green oasis amidst the metropolis, creating a sensation of privacy and tranquility.
