Everything you were
going to ask at the door.

Do I need experience? +

For PD routes: fitness, a head for heights, and honesty about both. The rest we teach on the way up.

What if the weather turns? +

We turn with it. Four percent of our ropes come home without a summit — all of them come home. Rebooking costs nothing.

What does the tariff include? +

Guiding, planning, and hut reservations. Lifts, insurance, and blister plasters are yours.

How fit is fit enough? +

Eight hundred meters of ascent with a light pack, at a pace where you can still hold a conversation.

Can I borrow gear? +

Boots, crampons, axe, harness — the bureau lends. Bring your own legs.
